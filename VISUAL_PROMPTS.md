# Original Atlas visual assets

Generation method: built-in image-generation tool. Generated originals were preserved; the project includes optimized WebP versions encoded with Sharp. No Icomat imagery was downloaded or reused.

## intelligence-core.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-bleed cinematic website hero for Atlas, a premium AI research platform. Create a stunning photorealistic 3D scientific sculpture: a vast floating toroidal neural structure made from hundreds of impossibly fine brushed titanium and translucent optical glass ribbons, elegantly flowing and twisting into a single luminous hollow core. Black graphite laboratory void, subtle mint green luminous filaments running along the edges, very restrained pale lavender reflections in glass. Form occupies center-right of a widescreen 16:9 landscape composition; generous deep black negative space at left and bottom for large white website headlines added separately. Material detail is exquisitely tactile, engineered aerospace precision with futuristic intelligence aesthetic. Oblique three-quarter macro perspective, soft volumetric haze and restrained studio rim lighting, realistic reflections, beautiful shadow depth, sparse white particle-like data points near object only. Sophisticated museum installation meets next-generation AI technology. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no neon cyberpunk city, no cartoon, no busy background. High visual fidelity and editorial luxury.

## knowledge-layers.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic scroll-story visualization for Atlas AI research software. Widescreen 16:9 composition, museum-grade photorealistic 3D scientific instrument against pure near-black graphite space. A floating exploded stack of five thin translucent smoked-glass rectangular planes, diagonally oriented in isometric perspective, with a precise luminous mint-white beam passing vertically through their centers. Each plane contains delicately etched data circuits and tiny pearl-like evidence nodes; fine luminous pathways connect the layers into a coherent knowledge architecture. Brushed titanium edges, subtle lavender caustic reflections, realistic soft reflections and shadows. Stack centered with abundant negative space around it, pristine engineering precision, quiet premium industrial aesthetic, high-end contemporary architectural render, captivating but minimal. No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, UI, watermark, human figures, or neon background. Details sharp and tactile, art-directed dramatic studio lighting.
